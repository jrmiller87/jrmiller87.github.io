His Place in the Realm

Ülric serves House von Dubá in its Watch. He is not yet a knight, and there is no confirmed promotion beyond his current role.

He was formerly associated with the Syndicate, later known as the Order of the Golden Rose, but his current faction membership is House von Dubá alone.

His brother Crixus Vaelen is a weaponsmith, miner, and bowyer. Siobhan Vaelen practices alchemy, herbalism, and woodcutting.
