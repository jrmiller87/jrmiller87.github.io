His Place in the Realm

Ülric now serves House Rado after leaving House von Dubá. He is not yet a knight; his present House Rado rank has not been established in the record.

He was formerly associated with the Syndicate, later known as the Order of the Golden Rose, and subsequently served House von Dubá. His current allegiance is House Rado.

His brother Crixus Vaelen is a weaponsmith, miner, and bowyer. Siobhan Vaelen practices alchemy, herbalism, and woodcutting.
